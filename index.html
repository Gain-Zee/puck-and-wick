The Founder Story

Twenty Years on the Ice.
One Uncompromising Standard.

“In competitive hockey, games are won in the inches. Tape-to-tape precision, relentless locker-room discipline, and zero tolerance for cutting corners. When I hung up my competitive skates after two decades, I brought that exact obsession to hand-pouring clean soy candles.”

For twenty years, our founder lived inside the rink—from dawn practices on sub-zero ice to high-stakes tournament finals. On the ice, success is governed by relentless preparation: blade sharpness calibrated to the millimeter, stick flex dialed for explosive release, and total accountability to the locker room.

When transitioning from competitive athletics to artisan craft, the goal was not to make ordinary candles with synthetic fragrances or cheap paraffin wax imported from overseas refineries. Instead, Puck & Wick was forged in Loudoun County, Virginia to create luxury home fragrances and athlete gear care that uphold collegiate and professional standards of durability, clean air, and aesthetic refinement.

Every batch is hand-poured in small volumes, temp-checked with digital infrared probes, and cured for a full 14 days before boxing. Even our signature $98 Exclusive Boxes carry that heritage forward—priced in tribute to our founder’s very first competitive hockey jersey number (#98). Because whether you are stepping onto fresh sheet ice or striking a match in your living room, Quality Control is always #1.

The Four Pillars of Uncompromising Craft

We reject petroleum byproducts, zinc cores, and synthetic additives. Our four-pillar formulation protocol ensures clean-burning luxury from first spark to final burn.

01

100% American Clean-Burning Soy Wax

Harvested from renewable Midwest soybean farms. Biodegradable, non-toxic, and free from petroleum paraffin sludge. Burns 30–50% slower and cooler than paraffin, releasing zero toxic black soot into your home.

30-50% Slower Burn • Zero Paraffin
02

Phthalate-Free Fragrance Oils

Clean-scent certified and rigorously screened against California Prop 65 hazards. 100% free from phthalates, parabens, acute toxins, and carcinogens. Masterfully blended with natural therapeutic essential oils.

100% Phthalate-Free • IFRA Certified
03

Lead-Free Cotton Wicks & Burn Testing

Custom-braided organic cotton wicks calibrated specifically to the exact diameter, wax density, and fragrance oil load of each vessel. Eliminates candle tunneling, uneven burn pools, and flare-ups.

Zero Tunneling • Calibrated Melt Pool
04

Small-Batch Hand-Poured in Virginia

Hand-crafted in micro-batches in Loudoun County, VA under tight thermal tolerances: 185°F fragrance binding and 135°F slow pour. Cured for a mandatory 14 days before boxed packaging.

14-Day Mandatory Cure • Batch Tracked
